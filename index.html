🌐 Website Safety Checklist

✔ Check the website address carefully.
✔ Look for HTTPS (but remember it does not guarantee trustworthiness).
✔ Avoid suspicious links and unexpected downloads.
✔ Never share passwords or verification codes.
✔ Use trusted websites and official apps.

🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips

🔐 1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. A password manager can help you manage them.

📧 2. Avoid Phishing

Do not trust unexpected messages asking for passwords, money or personal information. Verify the sender independently.

🔄 3. Update Your Software

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated to receive security fixes.

🔒 4. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use an additional verification step to make your accounts more secure.

📶 5. Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive activities on untrusted networks and never ignore browser security warnings.
